Every few months someone asks what's actually in our spice drawer, expecting the answer to be enormous. It isn't. Eleven things cover almost everything on this site.

### Buy whole where you can

Ground spice loses its edge in weeks. Whole spice keeps for the better part of a year. Toast cumin and coriander in a dry pan for ninety seconds, grind them, and the difference is not subtle.
